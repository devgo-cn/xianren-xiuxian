# 全境界数值平衡模拟 (2026-09-17)

## 假设
- artMult=1.5 (装备加成), 无buff
- 打怪速率: 120秒打100只
- 离线打坐灵石: spiritRate × dt × 0.7

## 数值公式
- rateNow = 4 × realmMult × artMult × arrMult × buffMult
- realmMult = (bi+1)^2.05
- arrMult(lv) = 1 + sum(0.12 for k≤11, 0.06 for k≤21, 0.02 for k≤31)
- spiritRate = 0.5 + 0.34 × arrayLv
- arrayCost = 900 × (lv+1)^2.55
- 打怪灵石 = (30 + 5×lv) × jitter(0.6~1.4)
- 修为需求 = 4 × (bi+1)^3 × REALM_DAYS[bi]×86400 × segWeight
